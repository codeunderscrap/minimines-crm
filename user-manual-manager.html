BD Manager Manual

MiniMines CRM Team Oversight & Analytics Guide

1. Introduction

As a BD Manager, you have visibility over your entire assigned cluster of Associates. You can monitor their leads, intervene in opportunities, and review quotations before they go to the HOD.

2. Global Leads Dashboard

Monitor all leads being worked by your associates.

3. Opportunity Pipeline

Track the health and velocity of active deals across your team.

4. Quotation Reviews

Review and validate quotations drafted by associates before passing them to the HOD for final approval.

5. Products Reference

Check the available SKUs and materials.

6. Performance Analytics

Analyze team conversion rates and lead generation sources.
